This policy applies to all visitors of emicheck.in and complies with:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — to the extent applicable to visitors from the European Economic Area

Information We Collect

EMI Check does not require registration, login, or submission of any personal information. All calculations happen entirely within your browser — no form data is sent to our servers.

However, certain information may be collected automatically when you visit our website:

a) Automatically Collected Data (via third-party services)

  • IP address — collected by Google Analytics and Google AdSense to determine approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL (the page you came from)
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Date and time of your visit

b) Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our third-party partners (Google AdSense, Google Analytics) use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies. Details are provided in Section 4.

c) Data You Voluntarily Provide

We do not have any contact forms, comment sections, or user accounts. If you contact us by email (as listed in Section 10), we will receive only the information you choose to include in your message.

Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to the website owner.

Types of cookies used on EMI Check:

a) Strictly Necessary Cookies
EMI Check itself does not set any strictly necessary cookies. All calculator functions work without cookies.

b) Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting information anonymously. See Section 6 for details.

c) Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)
Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalised or contextual advertisements based on your browsing behaviour. See Section 5 for details.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the display of advertisements but will not affect the EMI calculator functionality. Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

You may also opt out of interest-based advertising across participating websites at www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu (EEA users).

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use EMI Check. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors.

Data collected by Google Analytics includes:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page
  • How you arrived at the website (search engine, direct link, referral)
  • Approximate geographic location (country and city, derived from IP address)
  • Device and browser information
  • New vs. returning visitor status

Google Analytics sets cookies (including _ga, _gid, and _gat) to distinguish users and throttle request rates. The information generated is transmitted to and stored on Google's servers.

We have enabled IP anonymisation in Google Analytics, which means your IP address is truncated before storage.

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data privacy overview.
